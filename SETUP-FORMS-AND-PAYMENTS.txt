1. WEB3FORMS  (contact, booking, and job application forms)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
a) Go to https://web3forms.com/
b) Enter TheArcadeRental@gmail.com and click Create Access Key
c) Check that inbox for the access key
d) Replace this placeholder everywhere it appears:
       PASTE-YOUR-WEB3FORMS-ACCESS-KEY-HERE
   with the real key.
   Affected files: contact.html, booking.html, careers.html,
                   FULL-SITE-PREVIEW.html
   (Find & Replace All in a text editor does all four at once.)

That's it — no account, no server, no monthly fee. Submissions arrive as
email in the Gmail inbox. Free tier covers 250 submissions/month.

Until the real key is in, forms do NOT fail silently — they show a
message telling the visitor to email or call instead.

Each form includes a hidden anti-spam "honeypot" field. Real visitors
never see it; bots that fill it are silently discarded.

2. PAYMENTS  (the 50% deposit on the booking page)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDED: SQUARE — Steve already has an account.

Set it up once in Square:
  Square Dashboard > Online Checkout > Create Payment Link
  > choose "Accept a payment"
  > turn ON "Let customer enter amount"
  > Save, then copy the link (looks like https://square.link/u/XXXXXXX)

Paste it into booking.html (and FULL-SITE-PREVIEW.html):
    var SQUARE_LINK  = "https://square.link/u/XXXXXXX";

Why buyer-entered amount: the deposit changes with package, extra hours,
attendant, and delivery distance, so a fixed-price link won't work. The
site shows the exact amount in big type with a COPY AMOUNT button, and
the customer types it on the Square page.

Square fee: 3.3% + 30c per online payment on the free plan (no monthly
fee). The Plus plan is $49/mo and drops it to 2.9% + 30c, which only
pays off above roughly $12,000/month in online card sales - not worth it
at current volume. Square charges NO chargeback fee, which is unusual
and genuinely valuable for a deposit-taking business.

OPTIONAL EXTRAS - fill in any, leave blank to hide:

    var ZELLE_HANDLE = "";   <- no fees at all, bank-to-bank

Zelle costs nothing but cannot be embedded - the customer sends the
amount manually from their banking app. The site displays the exact
amount and Steve's handle. Good as a "save us both the fee" option
next to Square.

Fee comparison on a $500 deposit:
    Zelle .................. $0
    Venmo business ......... $9.60   (1.9% + $0.10)
    Cash App business ...... $13.75  (2.75%)
    Square online .......... $16.80  (3.3% + $0.30)
    PayPal Checkout ........ $17.94  (3.49% + $0.49)

Square is not the cheapest, but it is the only one here that is a real
merchant account: proper receipts, refund handling, dispute support, and
it accepts cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Cash App Pay. For deposits
on four-figure bookings that is usually worth the few dollars.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT - READ BEFORE TAKING REAL MONEY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
* The deposit is calculated in the customer's browser and, with the
  buyer-entered-amount setup, typed in by the customer. ALWAYS confirm
  the amount received matches the quote before committing to a date.
  This is a normal manual check at this size, but it is a real
  limitation of any setup without a server.

* Do NOT use personal PayPal Friends & Family, or a personal Venmo or
  Cash App account, to dodge fees. It violates their terms and those
  platforms can freeze funds or close the account over it. Losing a
  season of deposits would cost far more than the fees.

* Any of these will issue a 1099-K once Steve receives $600+ in a year.
  This income is reportable regardless of which he picks.
