DELIVERY DRIVER — ARCADE TRAILER
Part-time, with room to grow into full-time

Tow our enclosed arcade trailer to events across the North Webster / Kokomo / Fort Wayne / Indianapolis area, back it in and set it up on-site, run light on-site attendant duty when booked, then hitch up and head home. Starting part-time, with hours expected to grow toward full-time as the business grows.

  • Proven experience towing a trailer — comfortable hitching, backing, and driving with one attached
  • Preferred: background delivering boats, campers, or other trailered equipment
  • Valid driver's license with a clean-enough record to be insurable to tow our trailer
  • Comfortable with heavy lifting (arcade cabinets are heavy) and basic hand tools
  • Reliable, punctual, and comfortable talking with clients and guests at events
  • Available evenings and weekends — that's when most events happen
  • Own reliable transportation to reach the shop/pickup location
GAME TECHNICIAN / MAINTENANCE
Part-time / As-needed

Keep the fleet running — troubleshoot classic arcade PCBs and monitors, handle routine cleaning and bulb/joystick/button replacement, and prep machines between rentals.

  • Basic electronics or general mechanical aptitude (arcade experience a plus, not required)
  • Comfortable working independently in a garage/shop setting
  • Valid driver's license to pick up/drop off equipment as needed

PRE-EMPLOYMENT SCREENING — PLEASE READ BEFORE APPLYING

As part of our hiring process, finalists for driving or equipment-handling roles will be asked to complete: a criminal background check, a motor vehicle record (driving history) check, and a pre-employment drug test. Offers of employment for these roles are contingent on satisfactory results.

Specifically, finalists will be asked to authorize and complete:

  • A criminal background check — a standard background screening covering criminal history.
  • A motor vehicle record (MVR) check — your driving history, since this role involves towing/driving our equipment.
  • A drug test — a pre-employment screening you'll be scheduled for if you advance in the process.

This is a general description of our screening process, not a legal disclosure document. Before any background check, MVR check, or drug test is run, you'll separately receive and sign the specific disclosure and authorization forms required by federal (FCRA) and Indiana law — those forms, not this page, are what legally authorize the checks.
