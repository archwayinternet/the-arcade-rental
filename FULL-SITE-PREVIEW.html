As part of our hiring process, finalists for driving or equipment-handling roles will be asked to complete: a criminal background check, a motor vehicle record (driving history) check, and a pre-employment drug test. Offers of employment for these roles are contingent on satisfactory results.

  • A criminal background check — a standard background screening covering criminal history.

This is a general description of our screening process, not a legal disclosure document.
